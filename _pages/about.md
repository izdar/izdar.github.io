I’m **Muhammad Daniyal Pirwani Dar**, a PhD student in Computer Science at **Stony Brook University**. My work focuses on:

- **Network protocol security**: uncovering vulnerabilities in wireless standards and internet protocols.
- **Internet measurement**: analyzing protocol adoption and performance across the web.
- **Accountability and auditing**: building tools to ensure fair behavior in complex systems like recommendation algorithms.

I previously was at the **University of Iowa**, where I worked on internet measurement problems such as measurement methodologies and system auditing.

If you're interested in my research or want to collaborate, feel free to [email me](mailto:mdar@cs.stonybrook.edu) or connect on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/izdar96).
